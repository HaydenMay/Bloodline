# Bloodline — Itch.io page draft

## Short description

Train, race, retire, and breed racehorses as you build a stable that lasts for generations.

## About this game

Every horse has a career. Your stable is the legacy you build around it.

Choose a young horse, shape its strengths through training, and decide how to ride it on race day. When its racing days are over, retire it into your bloodstock and breed the next generation. Follow the family tree to see how traits, hidden coat genes, and racing potential travel through your line.

Bloodline is a browser-first horse racing and breeding game focused on long-term stable building and race-by-race decisions.

### Features

- Race with pacing, fatigue, drafting, traffic, and timed kicks.
- Train a horse over a career, manage its condition, and choose when to race or rest.
- Retire horses into a persistent stable and breed future generations.
- Trace pedigrees and coat inheritance in the Archive.
- Meet a changing field of AI rivals across divisions.

## Early Access

Bloodline is being released in Early Access while the game continues to receive balance, interface, accessibility, and content work. The core stable, career, racing, and breeding loop is playable. Update this page with concrete changes and known limitations for each release.

## Controls and saves

The game runs in a modern desktop or mobile web browser. On race day, tap or click to kick and hold to take a pull; autopilot is available. Career data is stored in the browser used to play. Players should use **Back Up Save** before clearing browser data or moving to another device, then use **Restore Save** to transfer it.
